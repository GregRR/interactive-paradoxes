Hidden Structures in Chaos

Investigating statistical laws that govern seemingly random or chaotic datasets.

5. Benford's Law (The First-Digit Anomaly)

In naturally occurring datasets, the number 1 appears as the leading digit ~30% of the time. Human intuition expects an even ~11.1% spread.

6. The Inspection Paradox (Bus Stop Bias)

Buses arrive every 10 minutes on average. Adjust the "Chaos" slider to make buses bunch up. Click "Spawn Passenger" to see why you always land in the longest waits.
